Abdul Bari

Abdul Bari's YouTube channel is a strong resource for students who want clear explanations of difficult computer science topics. His teaching style is especially useful for Penn State students taking courses that involve algorithms, data structures, and systems-related concepts. Instead of only memorizing procedures, students can use his videos to understand why concepts work.

Many students benefit from hearing a second explanation after lecture. When a topic feels rushed or confusing in class, Abdul Bari's content can provide a more patient walkthrough. That can be valuable when studying for exams, finishing assignments, or reviewing before interviews.

How it helps a Penn State CS major

  • Breaks down challenging theory in a simpler way
  • Supports exam review and concept reinforcement
  • Helps students connect formulas, logic, and implementation
  • Useful for learning at your own pace outside lecture
