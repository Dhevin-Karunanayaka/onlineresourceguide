Coursera

Coursera is another useful online resource because it offers structured courses from universities and companies on computer science topics. For Penn State students, it can be helpful when they want guided learning beyond the classroom. Students can use it to review programming foundations, explore machine learning, learn databases, or strengthen software development skills.

Unlike short videos or isolated practice problems, Coursera often provides a full sequence of lessons, exercises, and assignments. That makes it helpful for filling knowledge gaps or learning an entirely new topic in an organized way. It is also useful during breaks, summer, or alongside personal projects.

How it helps a Penn State CS major

  • Provides structured learning on technical topics
  • Helps students explore areas not fully covered in class
  • Can support project building and career exploration
  • Useful for self-paced learning during the semester or summer
