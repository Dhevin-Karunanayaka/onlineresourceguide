Overview

Being successful as a computer science major at Penn State takes more than just passing classes. Students need to build strong problem-solving ability, understand technical concepts deeply, and stay consistent with practice outside the classroom. Online resources can help fill in gaps, reinforce difficult topics, and provide extra opportunities to grow.

This website focuses on six online resources that can help Penn State computer science students improve: LeetCode, Abdul Bari's YouTube channel, Coursera, GeeksforGeeks, Reddit, and GitHub. Each one supports a different part of the learning process. LeetCode helps with coding interview preparation and algorithmic thinking. Abdul Bari helps explain difficult concepts clearly. Coursera offers structured courses that can strengthen technical foundations and introduce new topics.

Why these resources matter

Penn State CS students often balance programming assignments, theory-heavy classes, projects, and internship preparation. Using outside resources can make it easier to review concepts, practice consistently, and build confidence over time.
